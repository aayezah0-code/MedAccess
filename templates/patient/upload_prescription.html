Tips for Clear Uploads

  • Doctor & Clinic Details: Ensure doctor's name, clinic letterhead, or registration number is visible.
  • Legible Medicine Names: Ensure the handwritten or printed names and dosages are clearly readable.
  • Good Lighting: Place your prescription on a flat surface in daylight or under direct light without heavy shadows.
  • Avoid Crop: Keep all four corners of the prescription paper within the frame.

Note: Uploaded prescriptions are securely stored under your account with status UPLOADED. Pharmacists will verify the prescription details before dispensing medications.
